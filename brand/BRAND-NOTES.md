# Brand notes

There is no formal brand guide. These notes collect what the studio's own material shows, so the
site can follow it. Sources:

- `logo/Achates360_LogoRGB.pdf`: the one-page corporate sheet (colours, both logo lockups, tagline).
- *Achates360 Portfolio Update, March 2026* (sales deck, PDF, kept locally and not committed: it
  shows other companies' work).

## Logo

- Two lockups: **horizontal** (figures left of the wordmark) and **stacked** (wordmark under the
  figures). Clean SVGs of both are in `logo/`, outlined from the corporate sheet.
- The site uses the horizontal lockup in the header and footer, drawn in one colour (`currentColor`),
  matching how the deck uses it (white on grey, white on turquoise).
- The wordmark is lowercase and widely tracked: "achates 360". In running text the deck writes
  "Achates 360".

## Corporate colours (from the corporate sheet)

| Name | Pantone | RGB | Hex |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lime green | 373 U | 203 232 128 | `#cbe880` |
| Turquoise | 324 U | 153 217 217 | `#99d9d9` |
| Orange | 1495 U | 255 144 21 | `#ff9015` |

The logo JPGs also come in black and purple. The deck itself leans on turquoise (cover), a deeper
teal for headings (about `#2e8a9a`), mid-grey title bars and yellow icon accents.

## Type

- Corporate typeface: **Officina Sans / Officina Serif** (ITC, commercial). The files in the studio
  folder are desktop fonts; using them on the web needs a web licence, so they are not in this repo.
- The deck is set in a system sans (Segoe UI / Calibri), not Officina.

## Voice and facts the deck states

- Tagline (corporate sheet): "faithful friend • thoughtful design".
- "Founded in 2001, the name Achates 360 reflects our philosophy of being a 'faithful companion and
  trusted friend' to our clients."
- Service headings: Strategic Branding, Advertising & Marketing, Publications, Digital
  Communications, Graphic Design.
- "Serving local and global brands since 2001": a wall of 56 client logos.
- Awards: GDUSA American Graphic Design Award 2016; Gov, Hong Kong and New York Design Awards 2016
  Gold; London Design Awards 2016 Silver; A' Design Award 2017 Bronze; Prime Design Mark; HOW
  International Design Awards winner; Singapore Book Awards 2017 (Book of the Year, Best Illustrated
  Non-Fiction winner, Best Book Cover Design finalist); The Paperweight Awards 2014.
- Case studies in the deck: Mann+Hummel MH Innovates 2026, Safe Roads Singapore, Enabling Village,
  Frasers Heritage Wall, Samsung, Grab, Grohe, Kaplan MRT, DBS Private Bank AI imagery.

## Where the redesign differs (decisions for the Art Director / MD)

- Accent: the redesign uses `#ff4b1f` (orange-red). The brand orange is `#ff9015`.
- Palette: the redesign's peach `#ebd2c5` and charcoal come from the Wix site, not the corporate sheet.
  Turquoise and lime green are not used on the site yet.
- Type: the redesign uses Bebas Neue + DM Sans (free web fonts), not Officina.
