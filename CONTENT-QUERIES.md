# Content queries

Copy and structure are reproduced from the live Wix site **exactly**, including the items below.
Each needs a ruling from the Art Director / Managing Director; each is a one-line fix once decided.
Nothing here has been changed in the clone.

Source: `docs/export-log.md` (generated by the export), `scripts/export-wix/known-missing.json`
and side-by-side checks of the live site against the clone.
