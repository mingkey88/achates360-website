      For the inaugural Singapore Urban Mobility Week (SUMW) 2026, Achates 360 partnered with global rail leader Alstom to design, build, and manage their flagship exhibition showcase. Strategically positioned near the main entrance and exit of the key conference hall to capture high-volume delegate footfall, the booth translated Alstom’s smart, sustainable mobility vision into a dynamic physical experience—drawing over 14,000 visitors across the three-day event.

      **Design & Execution Rationale**

      Adhering closely to Alstom’s global brand design language, we introduced complementary natural wood finishes to create a contemporary, inviting aesthetic that softened industrial precision with human-centric warmth. Open sightlines and intuitive circulation pathways optimised traffic flow across a multi-functional footprint:

      -   **Five Interactive Zones:** Featuring bespoke digital applications developed in France, supported seamlessly by Achates 360’s dedicated on-site IT team.
          
      -   **Rail Stage Cinema Zone:** An open-format hub anchoring daily presentations, fireside chats, seminars, and thought-leadership sessions.
          
      -   **Reception Area:** Positioned at the forefront to welcome guests and streamline daily FAQ enquiries.
          
      -   **Coffee & Collaboration Zone:** A social magnet fostering partner networking while capturing visitor insights through survey redemptions.
          
      -   **Private Meeting Room:** An enclosed, quiet suite designed for confidential stakeholder discussions.
          

      To quantify spatial engagement, we installed a 4-point camera tracking system across the booth, recording more than 14,000 visitors over three days and validating the impact of our strategic placement and experience design.
