      Drawing its inspiration from the classic Hermes scarf to match the highest tier of LIXIL products, the key visual was created from product shots from DXV’s collections and animated into a moving kaleidoscope, to impress and wow its viewers from the onset. 

      The user experience was enhanced to allow users to view how DXV’s products would look like in their various product colours and see recommendations of complementary products from one collection to another for more control and customisation. 

      We designed the cover of the design inspiration book with monochrome colours and sleek styling for a look of understated glam that would enhance any of DXV’s showrooms.
