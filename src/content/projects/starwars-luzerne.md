This is our rendition of 5 stones featuring the 5 troopers from The Bad Batch. These patterns are inspired by the different fabrics used to make 5 stones. Using a mixture of patterns like batik, mandala, Chinese and Peranakan to relate to Singapore's diversity where cultures come together. The weapons are illustrated inside the 5 stones accordingly with the troops. Elements of Star Wars such as the Clone 99 logo and Havoc Marauder are also incorporated into this piece.
