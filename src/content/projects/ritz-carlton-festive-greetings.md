      The Ritz-Carlton, Millenia Singapore is a luxury hotel located in the heart of Marina Bay. Renowned for its panoramic views of the city skyline and exceptional hospitality, the hotel is also home to one of the largest contemporary art collections in Southeast Asia. With its refined interiors and distinctive service, the hotel offers guests an experience centred on comfort, elegance and quiet indulgence.

      **Celebrating the Seasons with Refined Design**

      Key festive periods such as Lunar New Year, Mid-Autumn Festival and Christmas are significant moments for the hotel, when seasonal offerings including festive delicacies, mooncakes and log cakes are presented to guests and patrons. These occasions are not only culinary highlights but also opportunities for the brand to express its signature sense of luxury and celebration.

      We were engaged to design a suite of festive campaign materials to promote these seasonal offerings. The scope included festive brochures, product tags, and both physical and digital posters that would communicate the hotel’s festive collections in a cohesive and visually compelling manner.

      Drawing from our experience working with luxury brands, the design approach focused on evoking a premium and celebratory atmosphere while remaining consistent with The Ritz-Carlton’s refined brand identity. Particular attention was given to material selection and print techniques, ensuring that each piece conveyed a sense of quality and craftsmanship appropriate for a luxury hospitality brand.

      The result was a set of campaign collaterals that elevated the presentation of the hotel’s seasonal offerings while reinforcing the elegance and prestige associated with The Ritz-Carlton name.
