    md: On 29 September 2026, the ALSTOM R151 Movia™ final train handover was celebrated with a vibrant ribbon-pull ceremony for 200 guests. An expert sound crew elevated the atmosphere, while attendees enjoyed Halal-certified French patisseries honoring ALSTOM’s heritage. To commemorate the milestone, a local artist painted the new train integrated into a Singaporean neighborhood. The artwork was featured on limited-edition EZ-Link cards gifted to all attendees, while the exclusive high-quality art prints were specially presented to the Directors of LTA and SMRT.
